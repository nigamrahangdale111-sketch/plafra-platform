Frequently Asked Questions

Are the ₹450, ₹3,500, and ₹8,500 prices per year?

Yes! The subscription cost is billed per year, while your creative deliverables (1-1-1, 2-2-2, or 4-4-4 Ad Videos, Templates & Banners) are delivered every month for 12 months.

What happens if I don't use my monthly quota in a month?

Your unused monthly quota rolls over into your Pending Available Balance and stays permanently available until your 1-year subscription ends.

How are refunds handled?

Online automated refunds are not applicable. For any special refund request, contact us personally via Business WhatsApp/Call and eligible refunds are processed personally via UPI ID.
